The project roadmap

01Beginner

SEO website audit

Diagnose technical and on-page issues on a small ecommerce site and prioritize fixes by impact.

Deliver: annotated audit + prioritized fix list

Scope: Crawl 20–50 URLs; inspect titles, descriptions, heading order, indexability, broken links, page speed, and mobile usability.

Measure: Issues found by severity; pages with unique titles; Core Web Vitals status; estimated organic opportunity.

Tools: Search Console, PageSpeed Insights, Screaming Frog.

02Beginner

30-day social campaign

Turn customer needs and content pillars into an Instagram calendar with a testable publishing plan.

Deliver: calendar, creative samples + scorecard

Scope: Plan 4 content pillars, 3 posts per week, stories, hooks, CTAs, and a small creative test. Mock the brand feed.

Measure: Reach, engagement rate, saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks, and follower growth. Label projections as hypothetical.

Tools: Canva, Google Sheets, Instagram Insights or clearly labeled sample data.

03Beginner

Website traffic analysis

Explain which channels and landing pages attract engaged visitors and support subscription intent.

Deliver: channel analysis + insight memo

Scope: Explore acquisition sources, landing pages, engagement, device mix, key events, and conversion paths using a demo dataset.

Measure: Engaged sessions, engagement rate, key-event rate, landing-page conversion, and source quality.

Tools: GA4 demo data, Looker Studio, Excel. Never present demo data as Northstar’s actual performance.

04Beginner

Keyword & content strategy

Map search intent to a focused content plan that supports discovery, comparison, and subscription.

Deliver: keyword map + 8-week brief plan

Scope: Collect seed terms, cluster by intent, review competing pages, estimate opportunity, and map each cluster to one page.

Measure: Intent coverage, topic relevance, difficulty-to-value rationale, internal links, and planned conversion path.

Tools: Google Trends, Keyword Planner, Sheets. Search volume estimates are directional, not guaranteed demand.

05Intermediate

Email performance analysis

Evaluate a sample campaign set and identify which messages lead to meaningful downstream action.

Deliver: cleaned dataset + campaign analysis

Scope: Standardize campaign names, validate denominators, segment by audience and message type, and compare performance over time.

Measure: Delivery, unique clicks, click rate, conversion rate, revenue per recipient, unsubscribe and complaint rates.

Tools: Mailchimp export, Python, Pandas. Opens are noisy due to privacy protections; treat them as a directional diagnostic.

06Intermediate

Paid media ROI dashboard

Give a clear view of spend efficiency, conversion quality, and return across campaigns.

Deliver: interactive dashboard + decision notes

Scope: Join channel, campaign, and date data; build consistent definitions; add date and channel controls; annotate optimization decisions.

Measure: Spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, conversions, CPA, revenue, and ROAS. State attribution window and source.

Tools: Ads sample data, Excel, Power BI or Looker Studio.

07Intermediate

Competitor marketing analysis

Compare three brands to find positioning gaps and a defensible opportunity for Northstar.

Deliver: comparison matrix + opportunity brief

Scope: Choose three direct or adjacent brands; capture dated evidence on search presence, content, social formats, and site journey.

Measure: Content coverage, engagement signals, offer clarity, mobile friction, and differentiating themes. Separate observation from inference.

Tools: Similarweb, Google Trends, spreadsheets, manual review.

08Advanced

Acquisition funnel analytics

Locate drop-off across visits, product consideration, signup, checkout, and purchase.

Deliver: funnel model + experiment recommendations

Scope: Define event semantics and user/session grain; build a stage funnel; segment by channel, device, and new/returning status.

Measure: Stage-to-stage conversion, absolute drop-off, time to convert, and segment differences.

Tools: Python, SQL, GA4, Power BI. A funnel shows association; experiments are needed to establish causation.

09Advanced

AI-powered customer segments

Build actionable customer groups from behavior and spend, then translate segments into relevant experiences.

Deliver: segmentation notebook + activation plan

Scope: Create a reproducible RFM dataset; handle missingness and outliers; compare interpretable clustering approaches; profile and name segments.

Measure: Segment stability, size, recency, frequency, monetary value, campaign response, and business actionability.

Tools: Python, Pandas, scikit-learn, SQL. Use synthetic or appropriately consented, de-identified data.

10Advanced

Marketing analytics platform

Bring imported data, campaign comparisons, performance flags, and recommendations into one usable product.

Deliver: deployed prototype + product walkthrough

Scope: Import CSV; validate schema; calculate KPIs; filter and compare campaigns; surface transparent rules-based alerts; export a summary.

Measure: Import success, calculation accuracy, task completion, response time, and recommendation acceptance in usability review.

Tools: React, FastAPI, SQL, Plotly. Show data provenance and the logic behind every recommendation.

Make results credible

One measurement standard

Good portfolio work shows how you reason, including the limits of the data.

Every case study includes

01 / Business questionWhat decision or outcome does this work support?
02 / Data & assumptionsWhere does the data come from? What is simulated?
03 / MethodWhat did you analyze, compare, or build?
04 / FindingWhat is the clearest evidence, and how strong is it?
05 / RecommendationWhat should happen next, and why?
06 / LimitationsWhat can’t this analysis establish?

Portfolio quality bar

ReproducibleReadable source files, formulas, definitions, and dated sources.
Decision-readyLead with a concise recommendation, not a screenshot gallery.
HonestLabel demo data and estimates; never imply fictional outcomes are real.
AccessibleReadable charts, clear units, useful titles, and no color-only meaning.
SpecificShow your individual contribution, trade-offs, and next experiment.
ConnectedReuse insights across projects so the portfolio tells one story.
